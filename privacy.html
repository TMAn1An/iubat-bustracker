IUBAT Bus Tracker

Privacy

How the bus tracker uses information.

Signing in

You sign in with your student ID and password. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes. To stop password guessing, we keep a short record of failed sign-in attempts (account and network address) for up to 24 hours.

Bus locations

Each bus phone sends the bus's location while it is on duty. We keep only the latest position of each bus, not a history.

If you share your location for a bus

  • Sharing is optional and starts only when you tap Share location and agree. You can stop at any time.
  • Your location is checked automatically (are you on that bus route and moving with the bus?). Only a checked location is used to move the bus on the map.
  • Other students see the bus, never your name or ID.
  • The transport office can see which student IDs are sharing, to prevent misuse, and can block sharing.
  • We keep only your last few minutes of positions while you share, and delete them when you stop or the bus reaches the end of the route.

Map

The map uses OpenStreetMap data served by OpenFreeMap. Your browser loads map images from them, which means they can see your network address, as with any website.
